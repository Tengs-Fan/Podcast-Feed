  • Post-training, one level down: how each method changes the weights 2026-11-16 · 8:46

  • If pixels are too noisy to predict, why does diffusion work? 2026-11-15 · 7:14

    JEPA says don't predict pixels; diffusion models do it all day. Why both are right: 'noisy' means uncertain, and language models, diffusion and JEPA each escape the blur differently (list, sample, delete). Plus latent diffusion, REPA and pixel-space diffusion.

  • The world model was fine, the planner was lost: 600 experiments on a JEPA world model 2026-11-15 · 9:14

    A deep dive into a 600-experiment study of the LeWM JEPA world model on Push-T: why perfect physics didn't help, why planning breaks past about 25 steps, and four training-free fixes (subgoals, a memory graph, symmetry, and ignoring the pusher). Based on public summaries of the write-up and the LeWM paper.
