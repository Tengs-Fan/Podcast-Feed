Privacy policy

This is a personal tool. Its owner uses it to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

It uses YouTube API Services with one permission: uploading videos to the owner's channel. It does not read, change or delete any other account data.

What it stores

It stores the sign-in credential the owner granted, so that later uploads don't need a new sign-in. It collects no information about anyone else and shares nothing with third parties.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
